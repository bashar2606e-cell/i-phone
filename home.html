Apple iPhone Series Overview

From iPhone X (Gold/Classic) up to the futuristic iPhone 18 lineup

iPhone X

2017
  • Display: 5.8" Super Retina OLED
  • Processor: Apple A11 Bionic
  • Camera: Dual 12 MP Rear / 7 MP Front
  • Special: Introduction of Face ID & Notch
Used Market (PKR): 25,000 - 32,000

iPhone XS (Gold)

2018
  • Display: 5.8" Super Retina OLED (Gold Edition)
  • Processor: Apple A12 Bionic (7nm)
  • Camera: 12 MP Dual with Smart HDR
  • Special: Stainless Steel Frame, IP68 Water Resistance
Used Market (PKR): 28,000 - 35,000

iPhone 11 Series

2019
  • Display: 6.1" Liquid Retina IPS LCD
  • Processor: Apple A13 Bionic
  • Camera: Dual 12 MP (Ultra Wide added)
  • Special: Vibrant color options & Night Mode
Used Market (PKR): 60,000 - 90,000

iPhone 12 Series

2020
  • Display: 6.1" Super Retina XDR OLED
  • Processor: Apple A14 Bionic (5nm)
  • Connectivity: First 5G Apple Lineup
  • Special: Ceramic Shield front, MagSafe support
Used Market (PKR): 95,000 - 140,000

iPhone 13 Series

2021
  • Display: 6.1" OLED (Smaller Notch)
  • Processor: Apple A15 Bionic
  • Camera: Cinematic Mode & Sensor-shift OIS
  • Special: Greatly improved battery life
Market Range (PKR): 140,000 - 200,000+

iPhone 14 Series

2022
  • Display: 6.1" Super Retina XDR
  • Processor: A15 (Base) / A16 Bionic (Pro)
  • Special: Dynamic Island introduced on Pro models
  • Safety: Crash Detection & Emergency SOS via Satellite
Market Range (PKR): 180,000 - 270,000+

iPhone 15 Series

2023
  • Display: Dynamic Island standard across all models
  • Connectivity: Transition to USB Type-C Port
  • Processor: A16 Bionic / A17 Pro (Titanium build)
  • Camera: 48MP Main Camera standard
Market Range (PKR): 230,000 - 350,000+

iPhone 16 Series

2024
  • Processor: Apple A18 & A18 Pro chips
  • Special: Dedicated Camera Control button
  • AI: Built completely around Apple Intelligence
  • Design: Slimmer bezels and enhanced durability
Market Range (PKR): Premium Flagship Tier

iPhone 17 Series

2025
  • Display: ProMotion (120Hz) expanded to standard models
  • Processor: Next-gen 2nm/3nm advanced architecture
  • Camera: Advanced 24MP selfie camera upgrades
  • Special: Introduction of ultra-slim "Air" variant options
Market Range (PKR): Flagship Pricing

iPhone 18 Series

2026
  • Processor: Ultra-fast cutting-edge Silicon
  • Innovation: Under-display Face ID tech developments
  • AI & Ecosystem: Deeply integrated generative intelligence
  • Special: Pinnacle of Apple's modern mobile engineering
Market Range (PKR): Next-Gen Flagship
